Speak your language.
Be heard in theirs.

G-One takes one spoken turn, turns it into the other person's language and voice, and can hand each person private help that the other never receives. It is a working prototype, not a finished product.

Try the live demo Read the limits first

  • Turn by turn One person speaks, stops, then the other hears the translation.
  • Private by seat Some messages go to one person only.
  • Voice-only yes An agreement needs a spoken yes from each person. No button.

      How one turn moves

      A diagram of the flow that is built. It is not live; the rail above is.

      1. 1SpeakThe browser records up to 30 seconds and sends it as audio.
      2. 2TranscribeGnani Prisma turns the audio into text.
      3. 3TranslateA language model translates and writes a form that is easy to speak.
      4. 4VoiceGnani Timbre speaks the translation.
      5. 5ListenOnly the other person receives the voice. Both see the text.

      Private lane. After a buyer turn, separate checks may send the farmer, and only the farmer, a dated price reference or a caution. They never block or delay the translated turn.

      Featured use case: farmer and buyer

      A farmer and a buyer who do not share a language negotiate a price. This is the first conversation G-One is being tried on, not the limit of the idea. Status below is as recorded on 7 November 2026.

      Translated turns

      Built · tried on two real phones

      English to Hindi and Hindi to English, several-second turns, 6 November 2026. Very short turns sometimes need a retry. Stop-to-audible time is not yet measured, so we do not call it real time.

      Private advice and caution

      Built · not yet tried on the live model

      After a clear buyer offer the farmer can see an AGMARKNET reference price for Common rice at Burdwan APMC, with its date. After a buyer turn that a classifier calls a threat, the farmer gets a fixed-wording caution. The classifier can miss threats and can be wrong. No caution does not mean the talk is safe.

      Agreement by voice

      Built · tested against fakes only

      The farmer accepts, both phones play the same terms back, and each person says yes. Two yes replies produce a nonbinding summary. The WhatsApp copy is not connected, so the phones say "not sent". Nobody's identity is checked.

      Where else this pattern could go

      Ideas only. None of these is built, tested or promised.

      • Service desks A clerk and a visitor who speak different languages.
      • Rental and small trade Two people fixing a price or a date by voice.
      • Field visits A health or extension worker talking with a family, with notes only the worker sees.

      Limits, said plainly

      • This is two browsers talking to one server. It is not a phone call and does not work on any phone line.
      • Each turn is translated after you stop speaking. It is not instant.
      • Speech recognition can mishear a number or a yes. The spoken read-back is the only check.
      • A price shown is a dated reference from AGMARKNET, not today's rate. It does not say what is fair.
      • A summary is a nonbinding record of two spoken yes replies. It is not a contract or a payment instruction.
      • Rooms live in the server's memory and disappear on restart or after sitting idle.
      • Use made-up names and examples. Do not record real people.
