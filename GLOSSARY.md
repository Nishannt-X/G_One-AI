# G-One hackathon glossary

- **Turn:** One speaker's recording from Tap Start to Tap Stop, capped at 30 seconds for the first demo. Translation begins after Stop.
- **Speaker A / Speaker B:** The two participants in one demo session. These labels identify people, not fixed languages.
- **Farmer / Buyer:** The two roles in the farmer–buyer demo; they replace the A/B labels. The creator chooses one and the joiner receives the other. Only the farmer receives the private market advisory. Either role can receive a private caution.
- **Private caution:** A fixed-wording notice, sent to one person only, after a turn in which the classifier saw a threat or pressure (told to the listener), a request for a secret (told to the listener), or a secret said aloud (told to the speaker). The translated speech is still delivered unchanged; the app does not block, drop or verify anything, and a caution never repeats what was said.
- **Secret (for cautions):** Only an OTP or one-time code, a PIN, a password, a card CVV or a full 12-digit Aadhaar number. A UPI ID, bank account number, IFSC code, phone number or name is not a secret here, because people give those to be paid.
- **Dated reference:** The farmer-only statement "AGMARKNET listed ₹X per quintal for <crop, variety, market> on <date>". It is never today's rate, modal price or a fair-price claim.
- **Agreement summary:** A nonbinding WhatsApp message produced after both people confirm the same frozen terms by voice. It is not a contract or payment instruction. States and wording: [FARMER-CALL-07](FARMER-CALL-07-nov10-architecture-scope.md).
- **Source language:** The language the current speaker uses. The first demo pair remains to be selected.
- **Target language:** The language the other participant receives in text and synthesized speech.
- **Demo session:** A room created by one participant and joined by the other through a shared link. Each participant selects their own language.
- **Speech contract:** The request, response, format, and model settings used for Gnani Prisma transcription and Timbre synthesis. See [Choose the Gnani speech contract](WAYFINDER-01-gnani-speech-contract.md).
- **Audio transport:** How recorded speech and translated audio move between the two browsers and the backend. See [Choose the browser audio transport](WAYFINDER-02-browser-audio-transport.md).
